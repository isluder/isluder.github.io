Isaac Sluder

Ph.D. Student | Mechanical Engineering Researcher

Bridging the gap between advanced composite materials and data-driven systems engineering.

I am a 2nd year Ph.D. student and Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Akron's Advanced Metacomposite Laboratory, advised by Dr. Kwek Tze Tan. My work exists at the intersection of materials science, software architecture, and machine learning.

My foundational research investigated the hygrothermal degradation of 3D-printed composites (such as PA6 and PAHT-CF) through extensive environmental testing. Leveraging that domain expertise, my current primary research focuses on Automated Extraction of Polymer Degradation Data via Large Language Models. I build tools and algorithms to transform unstructured scientific literature into machine-readable knowledge, utilizing technologies like GraphRAG, Cypher, KuzuDB, and SQLite.

I am thrilled to be joining the Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL) at Wright-Patterson AFB as an incoming intern (summer 2026), where I look forward to contributing to aerospace material advancements. Long-term, my goal is to design intelligent systems that improve engineering decision-making through the integration of advanced databases, machine learning, and LLM pipelines.

When I step away from the lab or the terminal, you can usually find me training for my next half-marathon, designing outdoor boot camps with F3, dialing in the perfect home espresso, or tinkering with my Steam Deck. Feel free to explore my site to see my latest projects and publications.
